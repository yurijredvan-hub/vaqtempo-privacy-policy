Privacy Policy

1. Developer and purpose

Vaqtempo is an offline tool for recording work time and estimating earnings. Developer: qarat. Contact: yurijredvan@gmail.com. Effective date and policy version: 17 October 2026 / 2026-10-17.

2. Local data

Shift dates and times, breaks, labels, notes, rates, bonuses, tax settings, report periods and premium-rate rules, payment status, templates, recurring schedules, timer state and display settings are stored locally in private Android WebView storage. The version and local timestamp of acceptance of the terms and acknowledgement of this policy are stored separately on the device. There is no account or registration.

3. Transmission and services

Vaqtempo does not automatically send records to the developer or a server, sell them or share them with third parties. The current app has no Internet permission, advertising, analytics SDK or remote crash-reporting service. Android, the app store and the system WebView provider may independently process system or diagnostic information under their own settings and policies. Those are not developer-operated Vaqtempo services.

4. Import, export and security

JSON backups and CSV reports are created only at your request. You choose files and destinations through the system file picker. A selected cloud provider can process a file under its own policies; Vaqtempo does not automatically upload or synchronize files. Exported files are not encrypted and can contain financial information and notes. Protect them and share them only with intended recipients. Import replaces current work records and settings after confirmation and does not import legal acceptance. Vaqtempo adds no database encryption, password or biometric lock. Access to an unlocked or compromised device or exported files may expose records. Automatic Android app backup is disabled; manufacturer device-transfer services may behave differently according to device settings. Absolute security is not guaranteed. JSON backups contain work records and settings but exclude active timer state and legal acceptance. CSV is a report, not a restorable backup.

5. Retention and deletion

Local work records remain until you delete or reset them, replace them by import, clear application storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Resetting work data in Settings → Data keeps legal acceptance; clearing Android application storage or uninstalling removes it. Exported files and third-party copies must be deleted separately. You can view, edit and export local records; the developer cannot access or remotely delete them.

6. Contact, children and changes

If you voluntarily email the developer, your address, message and attachments are processed through the email provider to respond to your request and meet applicable retention obligations. Do not send work records unnecessarily. Contact the same address about that correspondence. Vaqtempo is intended for work and earnings records and is not directed to children; it has no remote collection mechanism for children’s data. Visiting a policy page on GitHub Pages is separate from the offline app and subject to GitHub’s practices. GitHub may process technical access data, such as IP addresses and browser information, when serving that website. The page contains no developer-added analytics, scripts or cookies. Changes to data practices will be reflected in the policy in the app and on its published page before introduction. Developer: qarat. Email: yurijredvan@gmail.com.

You supply and check rates, breaks, taxes, deductions, increased-rate intervals, schedules and payment status. Recurring entries are generated when the app is opened or relevant actions are taken; they are not autonomous background attendance tracking.

4. Time conventions

Entered local clock times and paid minutes are used. An end time at or before the start means the next day; equal shift times mean 24 hours, the maximum shift duration. Daylight-saving changes are not automatically corrected. A report period includes its start and excludes its end. Shifts crossing a period or month boundary are split by actual paid minutes, including night and premium pay. Bonuses are proportional to paid minutes. Cumulative rounding ensures adjacent portions add up to the whole shift, including percentage tax; details may include rounding adjustments. Fixed withholding is applied once per selected period, not per shift or each calendar month. Breaks are unpaid and are taken from the end unless a break start is specified. New premium rules may select weekdays, an exact date or a recurring day of the month; a missing day, such as 31 in February, is skipped. An overnight rule continues from its selected start day; equal rule times mean that whole calendar day. When several premium rules match, the highest rate applies, never below base pay, and combines with night pay using the selected maximum or multiplication method. Previously saved weekly rules retain their calendar-minute meaning. Each shift keeps its saved pay rules until explicitly recalculated; recalculation updates whole overlapping shifts, including portions outside the selected period. Different currencies are not automatically converted. Check timer-derived dates and breaks before saving.

Software faults, operating-system restrictions, damaged storage or device loss may affect timers, records or calculations. Keep protected backups and independently check important results. No uninterrupted availability or error-free result is promised. Import can replace data after confirmation; uninstalling or clearing storage can remove local records. Software and design are protected by applicable intellectual-property law; you retain rights to your records. Use the app lawfully without infringing others’ rights. To the extent allowed by law, the app is provided as available without guarantees of fitness for a particular payroll or tax purpose; the developer is not responsible for losses from unverified estimates or unbacked-up records.
